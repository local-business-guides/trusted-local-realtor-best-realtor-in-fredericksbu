What to ask and how the options work

If you’re evaluating a franchise, ask the company to explain the franchise relationship, what services are included, what costs apply, and what ongoing obligations you would take on. Timing can depend on the arrangement and the steps involved, so ask for a clear outline rather than relying on a general estimate. Compare written terms and make sure you understand which business is responsible for each part.

If you’re selling a home, the comparison is different. We can discuss a direct cash sale and compare it with a possible listing. The amount you might net from listing can be affected by the home’s condition, preparation, and related costs. A direct sale avoids the need for repairs or cleaning under our stated service, but you should review the details of any offer before deciding.

Useful terms to know:
- Franchise: a business arrangement that allows a company to operate using another company’s brand and system under agreed terms.
- Franchise fee: a charge associated with entering a franchise arrangement. Ask the franchisor to explain all charges.
- Net proceeds: what remains after applicable costs are accounted for in a sale.
