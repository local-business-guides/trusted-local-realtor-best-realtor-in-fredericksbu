What the process covers and how to compare options

Start by confirming what kind of property you have and what service you need. A commercial broker may handle a different type of property and transaction than a company that buys houses. Ask each provider to describe its role in writing, explain any costs, and outline the steps and expected timing. Don’t assume that two businesses using similar real estate language offer the same service.

For a home sale, we can discuss the property and your circumstances, then compare a direct cash offer with a possible agent-listed sale. The net amount from listing can depend on the home, its condition, preparation, and the costs involved. We can help you look at the comparison, but the right choice depends on your priorities and the figures available to you.

A few terms you may hear:
- Direct cash sale: selling the home to a buyer without listing it for sale through an agent.
- Commission: compensation paid to an agent under an agreement.
- Net proceeds: the amount left after applicable costs are taken into account.
